# Event Ticketing and Check-in Problem Statement

Prepared by: Luma Chrisparr Ngale Jomia  
Matricule: FE25A114  
Role: Analysis Lead

Churches and student associations often organise events, but may face difficulties managing attendance:

1. Registration information can be incomplete, outdated or spread across multiple records, so organisers may not have one reliable attendee list.
2. Organisers may not know how many people to expect, making it harder to plan seating, food, programmes and the venue.
3. Ticket payment records may not clearly show who has paid, who has not and how much money was collected.
4. Tickets may not be checked consistently at the entrance, allowing reuse and making attendance counts unreliable.
5. Organisers may struggle to notify attendees about event changes and to produce reliable reports on past events.
