FIELD NOTES - PROVISION SHOP
Student name: Noela namondo kange
Business Type: Provision shop
Observation Area: Stock Management, Sales and Credit Management
Stock Management
The shop owner manages her stock manually using a notebook and pen. she visits the market every Tuestays and fridays to purchase new products.
every Thursday evening, she checks the shop to identify products that are running lowor have finished. she writes down the products that need to be replaced before going to the market.
Sales management
For normal cash sales, customers select the products they want, make payment, and receive their goods. These transaction are not recorded in a book. She keeps evreything in her mine
when it comes to sales of products.
Credit Management
for customers who buy goods on credit, the shop owner records their names and the products they collected in a notebook. Customer are expected to pay their outstanding debt later, usually after receiving 
their salaries. Whenever a customer collects goods without making payment, the owner writes down the transaction.
Challenges Observed
the shop relies mainly on handwritten records. sometimes, the notebook gets missing or erased, the notes are unorganized or some pages are damage or erased.
this can cause confusion when checking customer's outstanding debts and keeping track of previous transactions.
Conclusion
the observation shows that the manual stock credit management can be challenging. A computerized stock management system would help the shop owner monitor stock levels, record sales, manage customer debts 
and keep accurate records of the transactions.
