# Field Notes
**Student Name:** MENGUEPTCHI NJAKOU KRYS-ALAN (FE25A130)  
**Group:** Group 10  
**Project:** Event Ticketing and Check-in System  
**Source:** Interview with Coordinator of Buea Youth Tech & Career Fair, Cameroon  

---

### How Registration Worked For Them
The event is shared on student WhatsApp groups and LinkedIn. People sign up on an online form with their name, phone number, email, and one morning workshop choice (Coding, Design, or Marketing). The team copies these details into an Excel sheet and sends a plain confirmation message. There is no ticket or ID number.

### Payment
The main fair is free, but the afternoon Startup Pitch session costs a small fee. People pay through MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money and send a screenshot on WhatsApp. One volunteer matches the screenshots to the Excel sheet at night. Many students pay using a parent’s or friend’s phone, so the volunteer has to call around to know who paid.

### At the Entrance
Three volunteers run the gate: one welcomes guests, one searches names on a laptop, and one fixes payment issues. They type each name into Excel to find it and mark the person present.

### Problems Reported
* **Long queues and tired staff:** Over 200 students arrive at once in the morning. Typing names one by one on one laptop causes long lines in the sun and stresses the volunteers.
* **Wrong workshop rooms:** Without clear passes, many people enter the Coding room even if they picked Marketing, leaving registered students without seats.
* **Embarrassing payment delays:** Students who paid with a relative's phone get held up at the door while staff scroll through old WhatsApp chats to verify payment.
* **Wrong food and seat counts:** Organisers do not know the real attendance early enough, so lunch packs are either wasted or not enough.
* **Missed job follow-ups:** Companies ask for a list of people who attended, but paper notes and Excel files are messy, so some students miss internship emails.

### Numbers
About 480 people registered, about 390 attended (including 65 walk-ins), and 140 paid for the Pitch session. Three volunteers worked at the door.

### What the Organiser Wants
Every registered person should get a digital ticket with a QR code showing their name, workshop room, and Pitch session status. At the door, volunteers can scan the QR code with their phones to welcome the guest by name and mark them present in seconds.

### What This Means for Our System
* **Unique QR tickets:** Create a QR ticket after sign-up that shows the person's name, workshop room, and payment status.
* **Phone scanning:** Let multiple volunteers scan tickets using their phones so lines move fast.
* **Easy payment matching:** Give each person a short booking code to include when paying, even if they use someone else's phone.
* **Live attendance count:** Show real-time numbers for each room so organisers can manage seats and food easily.
* **Clean contact list:** Export a clean list of checked-in attendees so recruiters can send job updates after the event.
