## Before the event

They estimate turnout from the current registration count, though fewer people often attend. No example or figures were given, so we still need to find out how large the gap is and why it happens. Some events are paid, and the organiser checks payments against paper lists or receipts.

## At the entrance

Staff use QR codes or barcodes to check people in and record attendance. The organiser wasn't sure whether tickets had ever been reused or shared. Queues usually take less than five minutes, and attendees rarely miss important updates.

## After the event

Past attendance records are kept in spreadsheets. The figures are usually there, but it takes effort to find them. When asked what they would most like to improve, the organiser chose reliable reports from past events. That sounds like the main problem raised in this response.

## What to check next

One answer needs clarification. Asked how many events they organise and how many people usually attend, the organiser wrote “None for now.” Since they also answered questions about payment and check-in, we should ask what they meant. It would also help to find out which report figures are hard to get and whether check-in data goes into the spreadsheets. The open questions were left blank.
