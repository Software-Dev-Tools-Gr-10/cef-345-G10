Event Ticketing and Check-in System

**Source:** My comparison of Ayamba Ebob Precious's interview notes and Tabe Miracle Fiagmenyi's interview notes. These are their interviews, not a visit I conducted.

## How registration worked

Ayamba's organiser used a Google Form or WhatsApp, then copied names into Excel. Tabe's organiser took calls and WhatsApp messages and wrote names in a notebook. So both had to keep a separate list, and names could be missed or written differently.

## Payment and the entrance

Both organisers matched mobile-money payments to names by hand. At the entrance, Ayamba's team searched a printed list; Tabe's organiser had two helpers ticking names. Tabe's notes also mention two people sharing one ticket. Busy entry made checking slower and attendance counts less reliable.

## What this tells us

A shared attendee list, a unique ticket that cannot be reused, and quick name search would address the problems both interviews describe. We still need to ask the client how walk-ins are handled, when payment must be confirmed, and how guests should hear about event changes. The attendance figures in the two reports are estimates from different organisations.
